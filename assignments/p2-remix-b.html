Genre Remix: Option B

Option B is new this semester, so there are no former student examples of it yet. You can still look at the Option A examples to see what a remix looks like.

For Project 2, you will remix one of the genre samples from Project 1 into a new genre. The new genre should have a clear audience and purpose.

Your new genre must:

  • include writing (i.e. it cannot be a visual-only genre),
  • include a design or visual element,
  • be written to a clear and logical audience,
  • be written for a clear and logical purpose,
  • have a different audience than the original genre sample, and
  • be printable and able to be displayed on a tri-fold poster (i.e. no videos).

Possible Genres

Students write about many different genres for Project 1, so there are more possible genres to remix into than I could list here. Keep the following in mind:

  1. Pick a genre that works for your content. If your original genre was a GymTok instructional video, then you'll need to pick a genre that works for instruction or teaching.
  2. The new genre has to be substantially different than the original genre. In other words, you can't remix a Reddit post into a Facebook post where there are minimal changes.

Remix Requirements

Content: The remix should...

  • Include the major information from your original genre sample
  • It does not need to include everything from your genre sample, and you may need to add additional information to fill in the content.
    • For example, if you are remixing a TikTok video into a blog article, you'll need to add more detail or examples or information in order to have enough to write about. That's fine! It just needs to be primarily about the content from the video.

Writing: The remix should...

  • Be written in the tone and style of the new genre.
  • Think about formality, style, grammar, etc. (For example, a blog article might have a more conversational tone and speak directly to the reader, whereas a news article would not).
  • Demonstrate expected conventions of the new genre.
  • Think about paragraphing (and paragraph length), titles, headers, style, order of information, etc.

For example, a news article would be expected to have a large title that would grab readers attention, the date, a byline (i.e. the author's name, typically right before the date), etc.

Design: The remix should...

  • Follow the design conventions of the new genre.
  • Think about page layout, columns, font size (for titles, headers, and paragraph text), color, photos/imagery, and any other design elements that tend to be present in that genre.
  • Be polished and well designed.
  • This should look like a professional version (or publishable version) the new genre.

FAQs

Can I reuse information from my genre sample for this?

Yes! Project 2 should use the information from your genre sample as a starting place for the remix.

Can I add something to my remix that wasn’t in my genre sample?

Also yes! Depending on your new genre, you may need to add additional information.
