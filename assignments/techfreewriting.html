Tech-free Writing Sessions & Journal

Tech-free Writing (TFW) sessions are class time dedicated to writing skills, idea development, drafting, and revision without technology. We’ll also write with technology in this course, but these sessions help us slow down and think more deeply, so we’ll reserve time for that in class.

For TFW sessions, you’ll receive a notebook/journal to use. At the end of each TFW session, the journal is turned in and I will hold it from week to week.

See the Calendar for which class meetings include a TFW session.

Grading

What is graded is the writing in your TFW journal. We do freewrites at the start of many class meetings, and sometimes during a full TFW session as well, so the journal fills up across the semester rather than on a set schedule. Final evaluation happens after the last freewrite of the semester (around Week 14).

Excused freewrites and make-ups

How many freewrites we do changes from semester to semester and depends on how often your section meets, so the number you can miss without it affecting your grade depends on that too. The numbers below are minimums. If we do more freewrites than planned, more will be excused, not fewer.

  • If your section meets once a week, one missed freewrite is excused and one more can be made up.
  • If your section meets twice a week, at least two are excused and one or two more can be made up.

Excused freewrites account for illness and other things that come up. Make-ups happen in class on the TFW Make-up Day; see the Calendar for the date. I’ll confirm the numbers for your section once the semester is underway.

  • A: Missed no more than the number of excused freewrites. Participated fully; writing shows thoughtful engagement and effort.
  • B: Missed more than the number of excused freewrites. Participated fully; writing shows thoughtful engagement and effort.
  • C: Missed more than the number of excused freewrites. Mostly participated; writing does not always show thoughtful engagement and effort.
  • D: Frequently absent, left early, or arrived late. Participation and effort were inconsistent.
  • E: Frequently absent, left early, and/or arrived late. Participation was minimal; writing shows little effort or engagement with the session’s focus.
