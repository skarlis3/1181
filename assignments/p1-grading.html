Project 1 Grading

Project 1 Grade Breakdown

Project 1 is a weighted category in your course grade. It includes everything listed below. All components must be completed to receive your full Project 1 grade.

Component Points Grading
Rough Draft & Peer Review 1 pt Credit / No Credit
Reflection & Checklist 1 pt Credit / No Credit
Revised Draft 3 pts Full check scale (✓+, ✓, ✓−, NC)

Rough Draft & Peer Review

The rough draft and peer review are graded together on a credit/no credit basis and are worth 1 point as part of your Project 1 grade. You will receive full credit for submitting a draft that meets the minimum requirements on the Rough Draft page and completing peer review.

Reflection & Checklist

The Reflection & Checklist is graded on a credit/no credit basis as part of your Project 1 grade. You will receive full credit for completing the form thoughtfully and thoroughly.

Revised Draft

The revised draft is worth 3 points as part of your overall Project 1 grade. It is graded against the requirements on the Revised Draft page.

✓+ (3/3)
All requirements are met.
✓ (2/3)
Missing one of the requirements.
✓– (1/3)
Missing two or three of the requirements and/or the paper is substantially under the word count requirement.
NC (0/3)
(no credit)
Missing four or more of the requirements.

Note: Assignments that are missing major criteria will be graded accordingly. For example, a paper that is significantly off-topic, incomplete, and/or substantially under the word count may result in a no credit grade.

Individual Conference Requirement

You are also required to attend an individual conference with me as part of Project 1. The conference is where you will receive feedback on your revised draft — it is not a separate graded item, but your Project 1 grades will not be released until your conference is complete. If you need to reschedule due to an emergency, reach out to me as soon as possible so we can find another time.

For more information about how the check grading system works, see the Grading Policies & Info page.
