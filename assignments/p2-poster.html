Requirements Overview

Poster Content

The following must be included on your poster:

  • Original genre (for Option A: Your original genre analysis essay, for Option B: Your genre sample post/comment. See below for more information).
  • Genre remix — This should be fully displayed on the poster
  • Name of your community
  • Information about the new genre (the genre you remixed into)
  • New audience and new purpose for the remix
  • AI Statement of Use — Include a small statement at the bottom of your poster (I recommend the far left or far right bottom). If no AI was used, please include that information. Otherwise, just briefly explain how and where it was used.

Optional: Additional information about the community, the genre you analyzed, or anything else you want to include. Additional design elements like photos, graphics, etc.

Poster Presentation Requirements

The poster presentation requirements are as follows:

  • Be presented on a tri-fold poster board (36"x48", or close to it so that everything can fit; any color is fine; must be able to stand up on its own [i.e. no regular flat posters that require being taped on the wall])
  • The tri-fold poster board must include the following (printed out and glued/taped to the poster board):
  • Original genre (i.e. revised genre analysis essay OR the post/genre from your community; see below for more information)
  • Remix
  • Information about the new audience, purpose, and genre
  • The name of your community (I recommend putting this as the title at the top of your poster board)

The poster board should also demonstrate design principles.

This overall poster should look professional and design choices should support readability, clarity of information, and the "vibe"/aesthetic of your community.

Nothing should be handwritten (possible exception is the title of the whole poster if you are good at artistic lettering; otherwise, plan to print everything out).

Optional:

Anything else you think would help people understand the community or remix project, such as background information.

Including Your Original Genre

Option A

For Option A, you will include your original essay from Project 1 on the poster board. It can be stapled, attached so it can be flipped through, or printed smaller to fit on the board.

Option B

If you chose Option B, your poster also needs to show the original genre sample you remixed.

  • If your original genre sample is static (not a video), include a screenshot (or screenshots) of the whole post.
  • If your original genre sample is a video, include a screenshot of the video and a QR code that links to the video.

You can make a QR code with Adobe Express's free QR code generator.

Design & Format

  • Size: Use a tri-fold poster board. It will need to stand on its own, so a normal poster board will not work.
  • Visual rhetoric: The poster should be designed in a way that demonstrates a basic understanding of visual rhetoric (layout, thoughtful use of color, hierarchy, font choices, etc.). Design choices should support readability, clarity of information, and the "vibe"/aesthetic of your community.
  • Professional appearance: Nothing should be handwritten (possible exception is the title of the whole poster if you are good at artistic lettering; otherwise, plan to print everything out).
  • Image attribution: If you include any images that require attribution, include the attributions in small print below the image used (not all images require attribution. For example, memes would not include attribution. Check the licensing on anything you use when available).
  • Software: You may use PowerPoint, Canva, Adobe Illustrator, or any design software you are comfortable with to create your remix and design your poster elements.

FAQs

I don't have a printer; what should I do?

The library at Macomb has both black & white and color printers, which you can use for a small per page fee. Talk to someone at the library for help using the printers.

DO NOT wait until the last minute to print things out. There are many things that could go wrong at the last minute, and this project cannot be submitted late since you are presenting these in-person.

I'm really sick or had an emergency on the poster presentation day; what do I do?

While this is a required presentation, I understand that things happen. If you have an emergency (or major illness) on the day of the presentation, please contact me when you can and we can discuss options for an alternative to the presentation portion of the assignment. Please only use this option in an actual emergency and plan on being on time and present.
