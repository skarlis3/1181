Genre Remix Project — Simplified Instructions

This page is meant to be used as a simplified overview. Additional information and requirements can be found on the assignment pages.

Project 2 Checklist

Project 2 has two parts: the Genre Remix and the Poster Presentation.

☑️ Step 1: Choose Option A or Option B

Pick one option. Both options come from your Project 1.

Option A: Remix your Genre Analysis Essay

You remix your essay into a new genre.

Option B: Remix one of your genre samples

You remix one of the genre samples you used for Project 1 into a new genre.

🔍 Step 2: Pick a New Genre

Your new genre must:

  • include writing,
  • include a design or visual element, and
  • be printable (no videos).

If you chose Option A, the new genre must also have room for enough detail to remix the ideas from your essay.

If you chose Option B, the new genre must work for your content, and it must be substantially different from the original genre.

Not sure if a genre will work? Ask!

✏️ Step 3: Make the Remix

Content

Include the major information from your original genre.

Writing

  • Write for a clear audience and a clear purpose.
  • Write in the tone and style of the new genre.
  • If you chose Option A, your audience must be different from your essay’s audience.

Design

  • Make it look like a real, professional version of the new genre.

🖼️ Step 4: Make the Poster

Use a tri-fold poster board that stands up on its own. Print everything out. Put these on the poster:

  • Your original genre
  • Your remix
  • The name of your community
  • Information about the new genre, audience, and purpose
  • An AI Statement of Use

Bring your poster on the poster presentation day. See the Calendar for the date.
